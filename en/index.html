MyKitten Help

How do reminders work?

Every litter box, food stock and health care item has its own reminder, at the time you choose (Settings › Reminders). Notifications are scheduled on your iPhone: if they don't arrive, make sure they are allowed in Settings › Notifications › MyKitten. From a notification, “Done” or “I bought more” updates the app without opening it.

How is the wet and dry food stock calculated?

Enter each cat's daily portion and how much you have left: MyKitten counts down every day, including days without wet food. After shopping, tap “I bought more”. You can recount a stock at any time from its page.

Several cats, several litter boxes

Add your cats in the Cats tab and your litter boxes in the Litter boxes tab. Each cat has its own portion; stock can be shared or separate (Settings › Meals), and each litter box can be linked to specific cats.

Widgets and Apple Watch

Add MyKitten widgets to your Home Screen or Lock Screen: the “My cat” widget shows your cat's photo and what's coming up. On Apple Watch, the app and its complications show the days left and let you log a litter change or a purchase.

Share your household

iCloud sync is on by default. In Settings › iCloud & household sharing, invite someone (another Apple Account). You'll both see and update the same cats, litter boxes and stocks; when one of you changes the litter, the other's reminders adapt.

Back up or export my data

With iCloud sync (on by default), your data is backed up to your iCloud and follows you to a new iPhone. Settings › Export my data also creates a complete history (CSV) and each cat's health record (PDF).
