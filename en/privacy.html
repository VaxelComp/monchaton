MyKitten Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

MyKitten does not collect any personal data. The developer has no access to any information about you or your cats.

Your data

Your cats, their photos, your litter boxes, meals, purchases and health records are stored on your iPhone (and your Apple Watch), and in your private iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit service (sync is on by default and can be turned off in Settings › iCloud & household sharing). The developer cannot read them.

Household sharing

If you invite someone, your household data is shared only with the people you invited, through iCloud. You can stop sharing at any time.

Photos

The app only accesses the photos you pick for your cats. They stay on your devices (and in your iCloud unless you turn sync off).

No account, no ads, no tracking

The app works without signing up. It contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled directly on your iPhone. No server sends them.

Delete your data

Deleting the app erases its data from the device. To also erase the iCloud copy: iPhone Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage › MyKitten.

Contact

A question, a bug, an idea? Write to contact@vaxel.fr
